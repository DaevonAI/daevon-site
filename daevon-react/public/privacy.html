Privacy Policy

Product:Daevon (alpha) · **Provider:** Daevon Systems LLC
Effective date:July 15, 2026 · **Contact:** contact.daevonai@gmail.com

Summary

Daevon runs its project analysis locally on your machine. Your project's source code and files are not uploaded to us during normal use. Daevon contacts our hosted services only to verify your license/subscription, plus the website, payment, and support handling described below.

1. Local processing (your project data)

Daevon's scanning, analysis, health scoring, and reasoning run on your device. Your Unreal project's code, files, and structure are processed locally and are not sent to us or uploaded unless a feature explicitly says so and you agree to it. We do not silently upload your project data.

2. Local files and stored data

Daevon may create or store local files on your device — such as settings, entitlement/license cache data, logs, project analysis results, reports, Project Knowledge Core files, local memory files, or runtime state. These files stay on your device unless a feature clearly says it will upload or sync them and you agree to that. You are responsible for backups and deletion of local Daevon files on your own device.

3. Data sent to our hosted services

To run licensing and paid features, Daevon sends a limited set of data to our hosted services (currently hosted on Render):

  • a device identifier (DUID) that binds your license to your installation;
  • your subscription/activation token or current license status;
  • your Daevon client version and the time of the request;
  • your IP address, which our service receives with the request and uses to detect and limit repeated failed activation attempts. This is a security measure; we do not use it to profile or track you.

Your project source code is not sent for these checks.

We do not store your email address on our hosted services. Your email is held by Paddle as Merchant of Record (see §6).

4. Telemetry and crash reporting

Daevon does not send automatic crash reports, telemetry, analytics, usage counts, or project diagnostics from your device. (Our hosted service processes the license-verification requests described above and may keep standard server logs of those requests.)

5. Visiting our website

Our website (daevonai.com) is hosted by Netlify and served through Cloudflare, which sits in front of it as a proxy and content-delivery network.

  • Cloudflare processes every request to the site and necessarily receives your IP address and standard request information (browser type, page requested, timestamp) in order to route and secure the connection. It also provides us with aggregate traffic statistics — visitor counts and request volumes — which do not identify you individually.
  • Netlify hosts the site files and receives standard server request data.

We do not use advertising cookies, cross-site trackers, or third-party advertising networks on our website.

Early-access waitlist

If you enter your email address into the early-access form on our website, we store it using Netlify Forms so we can contact you when early access opens.

  • We use it only for that purpose, and to tell you about Daevon's availability.
  • We do not sell it, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.
  • You can ask us to remove you at any time by emailing contact.daevonai@gmail.com.

6. Subscriptions, payment, and support data

If you subscribe, activate a license, or contact us for support, we or our providers may process:

  • your email address, which is collected and held by Paddle as Merchant of Record for your purchase. We do not store your email address in our own systems. If you contact us for support or to re-issue a lost activation token, we look your subscription up through Paddle using that address;
  • your subscription and activation details — your license status, activation token, and bound device — held on our hosted service;
  • payment information, handled entirely by Paddle — we do not see or store your payment card details;
  • support/bug-report content you choose to send (see Section 7).

7. Support and bug reports

If you contact us for support or submit a bug report, we may receive the information you choose to send — such as your email address, message content, screenshots, logs, error details, device/license status, and any files or snippets you attach. Support email is currently received through Google (Gmail).

Please do not send private keys, API keys, passwords, activation tokens, confidential third-party code, or sensitive project files through support channels unless we specifically request them and you are authorized to share them.

8. What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not silently upload your local project files.
  • We do not use your project code to train AI models.
  • We do not use advertising cookies or cross-site tracking on our website.

10. Children

Daevon is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has given us information, email contact.daevonai@gmail.com and we will delete it.

11. International users and legal bases

Your information may be processed in the United States or other countries where we or our service providers operate; those countries may have data-protection laws different from where you live. For users in the EEA or UK, our legal bases for processing may include providing the software/service you requested, our legitimate interests in securing and improving Daevon, complying with legal obligations, and your consent where required (including for the waitlist).

12. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data by emailing contact.daevonai@gmail.com, and we will respond within a reasonable time. If you are in the EEA, UK, or California, you may have additional rights under laws such as the GDPR or CCPA (including access, deletion, and — where applicable — the right not to have personal data sold; we do not sell personal data).

Two things to know:

  • Deleting your subscription record ends your access. Your activation token identifies your subscription; if we delete that record, Daevon can no longer verify your license.
  • Deleting hosted data does not delete local files on your own device. You may need to remove local Daevon files yourself.

13. Data retention

We keep different data for different periods, based on what it's for:

  • Subscription, license, and activation records — kept for as long as your license is active, and for a reasonable period afterward so that we can restore access, honour refunds, and meet accounting obligations.
  • Waitlist email — kept until early access opens and we've contacted you, or until you ask us to remove you, whichever is first.
  • Support messages — kept for as long as needed to handle your request and for a reasonable period afterward for context on follow-ups.
  • Server logs — kept for a short period for security and debugging.

We may keep data longer where we must to meet a legal obligation. We use reasonable technical measures to protect data in transit and at rest. No system is perfectly secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

14. Third-party providers

  • Paddle — payment processing; Merchant of Record and seller of record for all orders.
  • Render — hosting of our entitlement/activation services and account data.
  • Netlify — hosting of daevonai.com and storage of early-access waitlist submissions.
  • Cloudflare — DNS, proxying, and security for daevonai.com.
  • Google — receipt of support email sent to our contact address.

Each has its own privacy practices; we recommend reviewing their policies.
