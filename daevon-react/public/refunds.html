Daevon is early alpha software. We would rather you have your money back than have you stuck with something that isn't working for you.

1. Who processes refunds

Our order process is conducted by our online reseller Paddle.com, which is the Merchant of Record for all our orders. Refunds are issued by Paddle back to your original payment method.

You can request a refund either by emailing us at contact.daevonai@gmail.com or by contacting Paddle directly. Either route works — we'll pass it on if you come to us. Paddle may also honour refund requests in line with their own terms and applicable consumer law, independently of this policy.

2. 14 days, no questions asked

If you ask for a refund within 14 days of your purchase, you'll get one. You don't need to give a reason, and we won't make you give one.

We may ask what went wrong — but only after your refund is already on its way, and only because it helps us make Daevon better. You're free to ignore it. It makes no difference to your refund.

3. After 14 days

Daevon is in active development, and things will break. If Daevon isn't working for you, tell us. We'd rather fix it than take your money for something that isn't doing its job.

If we can't fix it in a reasonable time, we'll refund you — regardless of how long ago you bought it.

4. If you can't access what you paid for

Your activation token is displayed once, immediately after payment. If you lose it, contact us and we'll re-issue it — we can identify your subscription through Paddle using the email address you purchased with. This is a manual process during the alpha, so please allow us a little time.

If we can't restore your access, we'll refund you. You should not be left having paid for something you can't use.

5. Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time through Paddle's customer portal, which you reach using the email address you purchased with. There's no cancellation fee and no notice period.

When you cancel, your access continues until the end of the period you've already paid for, and you won't be charged again. Cancelling doesn't automatically refund the current period — if you want that too, just ask.

6. What happens when we refund you

Your access to Daevon ends and your activation token is retired. If you subscribe again later, you'll receive a new one.

7. Chargebacks

If something's gone wrong, please contact us first. We will almost certainly just refund you, and it's faster than a chargeback for everyone. Raising a chargeback without contacting us may result in your access being suspended while it's resolved.
